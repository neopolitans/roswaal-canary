# Teensy Tiny Tankery models

The sixteen `.glb` files in this folder are tank models from **Teensy Tiny Tankery by MayLizbeth
("extraclear")**, licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(CC BY 4.0). The licence's full text is in
[`notices/upstream/teensy-tiny-tankery.txt`](../../../../../../notices/upstream/teensy-tiny-tankery.txt).

They are **not** covered by Roswaal's 0BSD licence. Reuse them under CC BY 4.0, crediting
MayLizbeth ("extraclear").

Roswaal Benchmark draws them on its Meshes station: each part a MeshPart, as a Roblox model would
have them.

## Changes from the original files

- Converted from the original `.fbx` files to binary glTF (`.glb`) in Blender 5.1, with each part's
  rotation and scale applied and no materials.
- Parts that carried third-party emblems from an anniversary event were left out: the Churchill VII's
  hull and turret emblems, the M26's hull and turret emblems, the T-34/76's flags, the T-34/85's
  emblems, and the Panzer IV's two symbols. The T-34/85's duplicate fuel drums and the T-34/76's two
  empty parts were left out too.
- The Panzer IV H is the event model without its symbols. The Panzer IV F2 is the H without its
  side skirts and turret skirts, as the model was built to be shown.
- The Challenger 2 was scaled by 2 and the Chieftain by 1.3, to the same scale as the rest of the set.
